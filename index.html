About Cristel

Food, fashion and beauty, served with love.

Cristel Monrose is a celebrity chef who brings the warmth of the family table to everything she touches. She cooks with celebrities on her show, teaches kids to love the kitchen, and carries the same glamour and generosity into fashion and beauty.

Her brand lives where a great meal meets a beautiful life: rosé on the table, a perfect red lip, and a story worth sharing.

Buon Appetito with Cristel is her celebrity cooking show — celebrities, cooking, and real talk. This site, Cooking With Cristel, is the social home for that table.

Mission

Make every day feel like a celebration at the table.

For people who love good food and a beautiful life, Cristel Monrose is the celebrity chef who makes both feel effortless, joyful and within reach.

Find her as @CookingWithCristel on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook.
